Create a complete, high-fidelity Figma website design for a fictional specialty cafe called KORA COFFEE CO.

This is for a university website development assignment. The design needs to look like a real professional cafe website created by a UI/UX designer, not like a generic AI-generated template.

Paragraph
Three cards
Button

- Full-width photography
- Asymmetrical image compositions
- Horizontal layouts
- Large whitespace
- Overlapping elements
- Different image ratios
- Editorial product layouts
- Small information blocks
- Large image-led sections
- Typography-led sections
- Horizontal scrolling concepts
- Interactive-looking components

Every section should look intentionally composed.

FINAL QUALITY BAR

Before finishing, review the entire design as a professional UI/UX portfolio project.

Check:

- Is the hierarchy clear?
- Does the homepage have a strong first impression?
- Does every section feel different?
- Is the design minimal without being empty?
- Are the images doing enough visual work?
- Is typography consistent?
- Is spacing consistent?
- Does the color system feel cohesive?
- Does the mobile design feel intentionally designed?
- Are interactions clear?
- Does the website feel like one complete brand?
- Does it feel original?
- Does it avoid looking like a copy of Stone Sip?
- Does it avoid looking like a generic cafe template?
- Does it avoid looking like an AI-generated design?
- Are Cormorant Garamond and Inter used consistently?
- Is there absolutely no Claude font being used?

Prioritize visual quality over adding unnecessary sections.

Make the final Figma design clean, professional, minimal, practical, editorial, and visually distinctive.

Do not make it a generic scrolling website.

The goal is a complete cafe digital experience with strong UI/UX, visual storytelling, and purposeful interaction.
